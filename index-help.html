Everything you need to know about browsing, ordering, and receiving your deliveries through the Neighbourhood Delivery app.

📝
Creating an Account
1
Open the app and tap Register
On the welcome screen, make sure the Register tab is selected at the top.
2
Enter your details
Fill in your full name and your WhatsApp phone number. Email is optional but useful for records. Enter your street address in the address field.
3
Pin your delivery location on the map
Tap the map button under Delivery Location. A map will open — drag the pin to your exact delivery spot, or search for your address using the search bar at the top. Tap Confirm when the pin is in the right place.
4
Tap Create Account
Your registration is submitted. Your account will be in a pending state until the admin approves it and sends you a verification code via WhatsApp.
✨ PIN your location precisely. Your delivery fee is calculated based on the distance from the shops to your pinned location. The more accurate your pin, the more accurate your delivery fee.
📱
Verifying Your Number
1
Wait for your WhatsApp message
Once the admin approves your account, you will receive a WhatsApp message containing a 6-digit verification code. This code expires after 30 minutes.
2
Enter the code in the app
Open the app and go to the verification screen. Type the 6 digits into the boxes — you can also paste the full code and it will fill automatically. The app will submit as soon as the last digit is entered.
3
Your account is now active
You will be taken directly to the shop. You can now browse products and place orders.
⏰ The verification code expires after 30 minutes. If yours expired, contact the admin and ask them to resend a new code.
🔑
Signing In

After your account is verified, you sign in using your phone number and the 6-digit PIN you received when you registered.

1
Tap Sign In on the welcome screen
Switch to the Sign In tab at the top of the welcome screen.
2
Enter your phone number and PIN
Use the same number you registered with. Your PIN is the 6-digit code assigned when you registered — check your original WhatsApp welcome message if you have forgotten it, or contact the admin to reset it.
💡 The app remembers your session for 24 hours. You will not need to sign in again unless you sign out manually or the session expires.
🏪
Browsing Products
🏪
Store Tabs
Swipe through the store tabs at the top to switch between shops. Each store shows only its own products.
📂
Category Pills
Filter products by category using the pills below the search bar. Tap All to show everything in the selected store.
🔍
Search
Type a product name or keyword into the search bar to filter the current store's products instantly.
📸
Product Detail
Tap any product card to see its full details — image, description, size, and price — before adding to your cart.
🛒
Your Cart

You can add products from multiple stores into a single cart. Everything gets delivered together in one run.

1
Tap a product to open its detail sheet
From here you can set the quantity using the + and − buttons, and choose what to do if the item is unavailable.
2
Set your substitution preference
Choose one of three options: Contact me first (default — the admin will WhatsApp you before substituting), Allow substitution (picker chooses a similar item), or Remove from order (item is simply dropped if unavailable).
3
Tap Add to Cart
The cart badge at the top of the screen updates immediately. Products already in your cart show a quantity badge on their card.
4
Review your cart anytime
Tap the cart icon or the Cart tab at the bottom. You can increase or decrease quantities directly in the cart list, or clear the cart entirely.
💾 Your cart is saved automatically — even if you close the app and come back later, your items will still be there.
✅
Placing an Order
1
Go to your cart and review your items
Check quantities, prices, and the estimated delivery fee at the bottom of the cart.
2
Select a delivery window
Choose from the available delivery slots. Each slot shows the day of the week and the time range. Slots fill up — book early to secure your preferred time.
3
Choose your payment method
Select either Cash on Delivery or EFT. If you choose EFT, the admin will send banking details via WhatsApp.
4
Add any notes and tap Place Order
Use the notes field for anything the delivery person should know — gate codes, parking instructions, or special requests. Then tap Place Order to confirm.
5
WhatsApp confirmation opens automatically
A WhatsApp message with your full order summary will open. The admin receives this as your order confirmation. Your cart is cleared automatically after a successful order.
🚚 Delivery fee explained: The fee is calculated based on the distance from our depot, to each store in your order, then to your delivery address, and back to the depot. All in one circuit. The fee is always rounded up to the nearest R5.
📋
Tracking Your Order

Tap the Orders tab at the bottom of the screen to see all your orders and their current status.

StatusWhat it means
PendingYour order has been received and is waiting for the admin to confirm it.
ConfirmedThe admin has accepted your order. It will be picked up on the scheduled delivery day.
PickingYour items are currently being shopped for at the store.
PackedYour order is packed and ready. You will receive a WhatsApp message with your till slip at this stage.
DeliveringYour order is on its way to you. Be ready to receive it.
DeliveredYour order has been delivered successfully.
IssueThere is a problem with your order. The admin will contact you via WhatsApp.
CancelledYour order has been cancelled.

WhatsApp Updates

We use WhatsApp for all important notifications. Make sure your phone number is correct and that you have WhatsApp installed.

🔑
Verification Code
Sent when your account is approved. Enter this code in the app to activate your account.
📋
Order Confirmation
Sent immediately after you place an order with your full item list and total.
📦
Status Updates
Sent each time your order moves to a new stage — confirmed, picking, packed, out for delivery, delivered.
🧾
Till Slip
Sent when your order is packed. Includes a photo of the till slip so you can verify what was purchased on your behalf.
💳
Payment
💵
Cash on Delivery
Pay the driver in cash when your order arrives. Have the exact amount ready — your total is shown on your order confirmation.
🏦
EFT
The admin will send banking details via WhatsApp. Please use your order ID as the payment reference so your payment can be matched quickly.
⚠ Payment covers both the cost of your goods and the delivery fee. The total shown at checkout is the final amount due.

Tap the Account tab at the bottom of the screen to view and manage your profile.

✓
View your profile details
See your registered name, phone number, email, and addresses on file.
📍
Update your delivery location
Tap Update delivery pin on map to move your delivery pin. This affects the delivery fee calculation for future orders.
→
Sign out
Tap Sign Out at the bottom of the account screen. You will need your phone number and PIN to sign back in.
📍
Delivery Address & Map

The map is used to set your exact delivery location. This is separate from your street address — it is a precise GPS pin used to calculate your delivery fee and guide the driver.

1
Tap the location button to open the map
Available during registration and in the Account screen. The map opens centred on your current GPS position.
2
Move the pin to your delivery spot
Drag the pin or tap anywhere on the map to move it. Use the search bar to find an address if needed. The GPS button in the bottom left will jump to your current location.
3
Tap Confirm
Your address is reverse-geocoded and shown below the map before you confirm. Make sure the description matches your location.
